# index.html — Elena & Marcos wedding invitation

Mode: Persuade. Redesign (visual-world replacement); product content, sections, and functionality from PRODUCT.md preserved exactly (fictional couple Elena & Marcos, 12 June 2027, Sevilla; hero/countdown, our story, the wedding, gallery, guest info, gift table, RSVP, FAQ, music, contact, footer). Audience: wedding-planning agencies evaluating the studio, and couples evaluating their own invitation. Success: first impression reads as agency-grade luxury craft, not a template.

## Direction contract

**THESIS**: Sevillian wrought iron (*rejería*) — the dense linear ironwork of the city's window grilles and courtyard gates — becomes the page's structural and decorative language, refusing the cream-paper-stationery default the previous build (and every wedding site in this category) reaches for.

**OWN-WORLD**: Warm lime-plaster white ground, near-black wrought iron for ink/structure, and ONE saturated accent carried at full confidence — a deep Sevilla terracotta/burnt-orange (courtyard geranium, cathedral brick), never a muted/timid gold. Iron-grille linework — thin, dense, geometric repeating patterns as actual SVG line patterns evoking rejería — frames sections, dividers, and cards instead of soft shadows or rounded corners. Display type: a confident high-contrast serif, cut sharp like iron scrollwork. Body/UI: a plain grotesk, no italic-script cliché anywhere.

**STORY**: Visitor lands and immediately sees the couple's names rendered as if wrought in iron against plaster — unmistakably Sevillian, not generic-elegant. Scrolls their story, ceremony/celebration details framed by grille-pattern dividers, gallery photography under iron-line frames, confirms RSVP through a form styled like a cast plaque.

**FIRST VIEWPORT**: Full-bleed hero photo (Sevilla architecture, golden hour) with a dense hairline iron-grille pattern overlay/frame at the edges — not a flat dark gradient — couple's names in the confident serif, terracotta accent carrying the eyebrow/date text.

**FORM**: Assigned direction, index 4 of 7 grounded candidates (wrought-iron rejería among: letterpress stationery, azulejo tilework, missal/hymnal typography, rejería [assigned], engraved calling-card, mantón de Manila embroidery, Mudéjar architectural plan). Seed key `05b94226`, mode persuade. Weighed against 6 dealt challengers (neo-Tokyo neon, Movida magazine, Łowicz papercut, iridescent cloud, drum machine, Ikeda data-sublime) — all six declined on both audience identification and product clarity (wrong cultural home, wrong mode, or wrong register for a Persuade wedding surface). Raises: Movida's committed full-saturation single-accent discipline (never a timid gold); Łowicz's layered-flat-shape depth discipline (depth through overlap, never gradient/shadow).

**FINISH**: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
